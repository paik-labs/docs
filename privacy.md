# Privacy policy

> **Draft — not yet in effect.** Last updated: 20 November 2026.

This policy describes how **LINA** (the "App"), an app for Atlassian Confluence and Jira Cloud provided by Paik Labs ("we"), handles data.

## 1. Where the App runs

LINA is built entirely on Atlassian Forge and is **Runs on Atlassian**: it runs on Atlassian's infrastructure and does not send any data outside Atlassian. We operate no servers of our own, and the App makes no calls to external services.

## 2. What data the App stores

The App stores, in Atlassian Forge Storage belonging to your Atlassian site:

- process templates created in the App (names, steps, ticket and e-mail settings, chosen people, groups, projects and spaces);
- for each page with a process: page and space identifiers, the current step, state and due dates, and the keys of Jira work items created by the process;
- a history of each process: who did what and when (Atlassian account IDs), and the reasons and comments people enter;
- space and app configuration (for example who may continue a step anyway).

On Confluence pages the App writes small content properties (the LINA badge and the document state) and, where a process says so, page comments. In Jira it creates work items and stores two technical identifiers on them.

The App does not read or store page content beyond the title, parent, author and labels of a page it needs to decide whether a process starts.

## 3. What we can see

We do not have access to your Confluence or Jira content or to the data in Forge Storage. Atlassian provides us with technical logs of the App (for example error messages and identifiers of pages, spaces and processes) so that we can fix problems, and with licensing and usage information through the Atlassian Marketplace.

## 4. Retention and deletion

Process history of finished processes is deleted after the period set in the App's configuration (12 months by default). Data in Forge Storage is handled by Atlassian when the App is uninstalled, according to Atlassian's Forge data retention rules.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell or share data with anyone. Atlassian hosts and processes the data as the platform provider; see the [Atlassian Privacy Policy](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy).
